# Privacy Policy for Habbi

**Last updated: September 27, 2026**

Habbi respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Habbi accesses and uses information when you use the app.

## Apple Health

Habbi can optionally connect to Apple Health to automatically track progress for Health-based habits.

With your permission, Habbi may read the following data from Apple Health:

- Steps
- Walking + Running Distance
- Active Energy
- Exercise Minutes

This information is used only to calculate and display progress for habits that you choose to connect to Apple Health.

Habbi does not sell Apple Health data or use it for advertising, marketing, or tracking.

Access to Apple Health is optional. You control which Health data Habbi can access, and you can change or revoke this permission at any time through the Health app or iOS Settings.

## Data Storage

Habbi processes your habit information and Health-based progress on your device. Habbi does not operate a server that receives your Apple Health data.

## Notifications

Habbi may request permission to send notifications for habit reminders. Notifications are optional and can be disabled at any time in iOS Settings.

## Tracking and Advertising

Habbi does not use your Apple Health data for advertising or tracking.

## Third Parties

Habbi does not sell Apple Health data or share it with advertising networks or data brokers.

## Your Choices

You can stop Habbi from accessing Apple Health data at any time by changing Habbi's permissions in the Health app or iOS Settings.

You can disable Habbi notifications through iOS Settings.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Habbi's features or data practices change. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
